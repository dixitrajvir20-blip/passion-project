About

Why this exists

Financial and business knowledge is passed down, not taught. If your family doesn't have it, school usually won't give it to you either.

The gap

Most advice about starting a business or managing money quietly assumes a starting point: savings to risk, a parent who has done it before, an adviser to call, or a network that opens doors. Young people without those things are left with social media content that is usually selling a course.

What we do differently

Every guide on LaunchPad is free, written in plain language, and assumes you are starting from zero. We use real numbers instead of theory, we say when something is difficult, and we never recommend a product we benefit from.

The content is written to make sense internationally. Banking access, currency stability and what counts as "normal" income vary enormously between countries, so guides explain the underlying principle rather than assuming one financial system.

Who it's for

People roughly aged 15 to 21 — old enough to earn, start something, or make a financial decision that matters, and young enough that these habits compound for decades.

The project

LaunchPad started as a college passion project. It is built and maintained openly, and it will stay free.
