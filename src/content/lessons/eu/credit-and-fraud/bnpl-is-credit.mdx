## A split price is a loan

At checkout a lender pays the shop in full, and you pay the lender back. That is <Term id="buy-now-pay-later">buy now, pay later</Term>, and it is credit. At 0% <Term id="interest">interest</Term> the debt costs nothing extra, but you still owe it. From 20 November 2026 EU rules require a <Term id="creditworthiness-check">creditworthiness check</Term> for most of these plans. A failed payment can still add a <Term id="late-fee">late fee</Term>.

## Plans stack on one date

Several plans at once is <Term id="loan-stacking">loan stacking</Term>, and their dates can land with the rent. Take every instalment due this month off your <Term id="net-pay">net pay</Term> after living costs, not just the newest one. List each plan in your <Term id="budget">budget</Term> as a debt. If one lands before pay, contact the provider before the date. An <Term id="overdraft">overdraft</Term> only moves the debt.
