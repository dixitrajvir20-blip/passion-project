## Fees are a cost of the job

A <Term id="platform-fee">platform fee</Term> comes off every booking: a flat sum, as here, or a <Term id="commission">commission</Term>, a percentage that grows with the price. With the card charge, fees take €5 of each €55. Like parts, they are paid on every job, so they are a <Term id="variable-cost">variable cost</Term>.

What parts and fees leave is the <Term id="contribution-margin">contribution margin</Term>. The €5 is about 9% of the price but 15% of the €33 left after parts.

## What pays the month

Your workshop space, insurance and tool loan are <Term id="fixed-cost">fixed costs</Term>, €320 a month. <Term id="break-even">Break-even</Term> is that divided by what one booking keeps, rounded up.

A monthly plan joins the €320; if it cuts the fee per booking, compare its charge with that saving times your usual bookings.

You cannot add a <Term id="surcharge">surcharge</Term> for an everyday EU consumer card, so the card charge goes in your price.
