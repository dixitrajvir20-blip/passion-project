## Why the same price keeps less abroad

Printing and packing cost the same wherever a poster goes. Postage, a <Term id="variable-cost">variable cost</Term> paid on every order, is €11 to Austria and €4 at home.

At the same €25, the extra €7 comes out of the sale's <Term id="margin">margin</Term>, the part that pays the month's <Term id="fixed-cost">fixed costs</Term>. One price with delivery included must carry the highest postage you pay.

## Returns and tax cross the border too

Online buyers across the EU have a <Term id="right-of-withdrawal">right of withdrawal</Term>: 14 days from delivery to cancel, no reason needed. They pay the return postage only if your page said so before the order.

Once your <Term id="distance-sale">distance sales</Term> to other EU countries pass €10,000 a year, <Term id="vat">VAT</Term> is due at the rate of the buyer's country. The <Term id="one-stop-shop">One Stop Shop</Term> lets one registration in your own country cover them all.
