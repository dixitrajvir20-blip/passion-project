## What each reply cost

Tell people about a plan and most will say they love it. They mean it. But praise is free, and nobody enjoys telling a friend that an idea will not work.

**Goodwill and a sale are different things, and only one of them pays for a stand.**

So weigh every reply by what it cost the person who gave it. A promise to buy one day costs nothing. An email for a waiting list takes a little effort. A <Term id="pre-sale">pre-sale</Term>, where someone pays a small deposit now, costs real money. That is the reply to trust.

## Three tests for this week

**Ask about the past, not the future.** Do not ask whether someone would buy. Ask what they bought last time instead, where, and what it cost. A past purchase is a fact; a promise is a guess. This is <Term id="market-research">market research</Term> at the size of one class.

**Pre-sell.** Show a photo or a sample, and take orders with a small deposit.

**Make one before you make many.** One candle, one notebook, one print. A first version like this is called a prototype. It shows the real cost, the real time and the faults while they are cheap to fix.

EntreComp, the EU's framework of enterprise skills, files early, planned testing of ideas and first versions under handling risk.

## Taking a deposit

A deposit is part of the price, paid now, for something you hand over later. Keep it small, so that saying yes is easy but not free. For a €12 candle, €5 is enough to tell a buyer from a kind friend.

Take it in a way that leaves a record: a bank transfer, or cash with a written receipt. Then note who paid, for what, and when they collect.

**A deposit is still the buyer's money until you deliver.** If you cannot make the thing, say so early and pay it back.

## Pre-selling online: what EU rules add

Sell online as a business, and two EU rules that protect buyers matter most for a pre-sale.

**Promise a delivery date you can keep.** With no date agreed, the seller has 30 days to deliver. If an order is late, the buyer can set a fair extra deadline, then cancel for a full refund. If the date mattered and the buyer said so, they can cancel straight away.

**Expect a few orders back.** Most goods bought online can be cancelled within 14 days of delivery, with no reason needed. That is the <Term id="right-of-withdrawal">right of withdrawal</Term>. Goods made to order or personalised are not covered.

Business registration and tax are national rules. Ask your country's tax authority before you sell regularly.

## Reading the result

The festival stand costs €90 for the weekend. That is a <Term id="fixed-cost">fixed cost</Term>: the same whether ten people stop or two hundred. The test lets you decide before you pay it.

A weak result is still an answer. Three deposits from forty people asked cost you a week, not €90. Change one thing, such as the price, the scent or who you ask, and test again. Or drop it and keep your money.

A strong result has limits too. Twelve deposits mean twelve buyers, not a crowd. Plan the first batch from what the test showed, with a few spares for the day.

## If the idea is food

Food brings rules, even at a stand that is up for one weekend. EU hygiene law is written for food businesses that run with some regularity and organisation. Each of those must register with the food authority where it works.

Whether a one-off school or festival stand counts is for the authorities in your country to decide. So ask the organiser what they need from food stands before you take a single deposit. If you plan to sell again, check with your local food authority.
