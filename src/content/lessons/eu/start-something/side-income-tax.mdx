## What the platform reports

Since 2023, platforms report their EU sellers once a year: <Term id="platform-reporting">platform reporting</Term>. The report names you and gives what you were paid and the <Term id="platform-fee">platform fee</Term> it kept. It reaches your tax authority, but it cannot show what the work cost you. Bus fares and worksheets are <Term id="business-expense">business expenses</Term>, costs of doing paid work.

## What your return asks for

A <Term id="tax-return">tax return</Term> asks for your income, reported or not, and the costs of earning it. Take the fee and your costs off what learners paid: what is left is the <Term id="net-profit">profit</Term>. In Ireland, the amount can change which form you file, not whether the income counts. <Term id="bookkeeping">Bookkeeping</Term>, a dated line per payment and cost, shows those costs.
