## Gross, deductions, net

Your contract quotes <Term id="gross-pay">gross pay</Term>, before anything comes off. Your account receives <Term id="net-pay">net pay</Term>, the only part you can spend. The <Term id="payslip">payslip</Term> lists each <Term id="deduction">deduction</Term> between them, and none is a fee the employer keeps.

## Who sets each line

Your <Term id="social-contributions">social contributions</Term> pay for a pension, health cover and support if you lose work. Income tax is an estimate of your year, taken monthly. Work part of a year and too much can come off; your tax authority settles it afterwards.

Your employer pays <Term id="employer-contributions">employer contributions</Term> on top, so the job costs more than your contract says. The country you work in sets every rate. Tax and both sets of contributions make up the <Term id="tax-wedge">tax wedge</Term>, which differs widely across the EU.
