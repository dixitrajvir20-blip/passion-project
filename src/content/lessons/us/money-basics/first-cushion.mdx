## What the bank did

When the bank covers a payment bigger than your <Term id="available-balance">available balance</Term>, that is an <Term id="overdraft">overdraft</Term>. Each item covered can carry an <Term id="overdraft-fee">overdraft fee</Term>. Your next <Term id="net-pay">net pay</Term> repays the shortfall and the fees first.

For a one-time debit card purchase, a bank cannot charge that fee unless you opted in; otherwise the card may be declined. Recurring bills, like the phone plan, can bring the fee either way.

## The first $400

An <Term id="emergency-fund">emergency fund</Term>, or cushion, pays a bill like the repair, so nothing goes overdrawn. In time it covers a month of <Term id="needs-and-wants">needs, not wants</Term>: rent share, phone, getting to work and food, here $1,055.

Start with $400, enough for this repair: $50 a paycheck, every two weeks, reaches it at the eighth, about four months.
