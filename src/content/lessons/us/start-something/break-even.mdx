## What one shirt leaves

The plain shirt, ink, bag and <Term id="platform-fee">platform fee</Term> come to $7.50, charged again on every shirt. Those are <Term id="variable-cost">variable costs</Term>. What is left of the $18, $10.50, is the shirt's <Term id="contribution-margin">contribution margin</Term>: the only part that can pay the month.

## Why sixty fell short

The press payment, design software and storage are <Term id="fixed-cost">fixed costs</Term>: $900 however many shirts you print. Sixty shirts leave $630 toward them, so the month ended $270 down.

The 86th shirt is <Term id="break-even">break-even</Term>, and each one after it adds $10.50 of <Term id="profit">profit</Term>. The press plan ends after twelve payments; then the count falls to 29.
