## Who pays the platform

The bakery paid $84 and $64 reached you, so the platform kept $20. It charged the bakery $4 on top, and took a <Term id="platform-fee">platform fee</Term> of 20% of your price. Both fees are examples.

The fees buy something: a client found for you, money held in <Term id="escrow">escrow</Term> until you delivered, and a written <Term id="scope-of-work">scope of work</Term>.

## The cut comes off the price

The platform takes 20% of the whole $80, before your own costs such as the $6 font. So the $16 is a bigger share of your <Term id="margin">margin</Term>, what the job leaves you, than of the price.

A <Term id="form-1099-k">Form 1099-K</Term> reports your jobs before fees, so keep your own record of each fee.
