## What one fee leaves

Each student pays ₹1,500 a month. About ₹300 of it goes on that student's printed notes, weekly test papers and folder. That is a <Term id="variable-cost">variable cost</Term>: twenty students need twenty sets.

What is left, ₹1,200, is the fee's <Term id="contribution-margin">contribution margin</Term>. Only this part can pay for the room.

## Why twelve fees are not enough

Rent, electricity and the projector instalment are <Term id="fixed-cost">fixed costs</Term>: ₹18,000 whether ten students come or thirty. Twelve fees match that figure, but those twelve students also used ₹3,600 of material.

Each ₹1,200 goes to the ₹18,000 first, so the fifteenth student is <Term id="break-even">break-even</Term>. Every student after that adds ₹1,200 of <Term id="profit">profit</Term>. The fixed costs start again next month.
