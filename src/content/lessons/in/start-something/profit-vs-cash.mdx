## Two sets of numbers, one month

A coaching centre teaches 24 students at ₹1,500 a month. That is ₹36,000 of <Term id="revenue">revenue</Term>. Four costs come out of it, and ₹10,800 is left as <Term id="profit">profit</Term>.

Rent, electricity and the loan instalment are <Term id="fixed-cost">fixed costs</Term>. They are the same whether 24 students turn up or 18. Printed material is a <Term id="variable-cost">variable cost</Term> of ₹300 a student, so it grows with the batch.

Rent alone takes a third of the fees. Electricity takes about what one student pays.

**Profit answers whether the month worked. It does not answer whether tomorrow's bill can be paid.**

## The 5th does not wait for the 20th

Twelve families pay in the first few days. The other twelve pay around the 20th, once their own salaries have arrived.

The bills keep their own dates. The press is paid on the 2nd. Rent, electricity and the instalment all fall due on the 5th, and together they come to ₹18,000, which is exactly what the early fees brought in.

The printing had already taken ₹7,200 of that. So the account is ₹7,200 short on the 5th, in a month that ends ₹10,800 ahead.

That distance between earning money and holding it is <Term id="cash-flow">cash flow</Term>.

## A cash plan is a list of dates

A profit calculation has no dates in it. A cash plan is the same month written as a calendar: what is due on which date, and what is expected to arrive before it.

Write the next four weeks in two dated columns. Wherever an outgoing line lands before the incoming one meant to cover it, you have found the problem early enough to move something.

The money kept back for those gaps is <Term id="working-capital">working capital</Term>. It is not profit, and it is not spare.

The other half of the plan is knowing who has paid. A dated, numbered receipt or <Term id="invoice">invoice</Term> for every fee answers that in seconds.

## What has to be registered

<Term id="gst-registration">GST registration</Term> is due once a business passes the turnover limit set in the law. For services that limit is ₹20 lakh in a year in most states, and ₹10 lakh in some. A centre taking ₹36,000 a month is at ₹4,32,000 a year, well inside it.

<Term id="udyam-registration">Udyam registration</Term>, the government's record of micro and small enterprises, is a separate thing. It is free, it is filed online with an Aadhaar number, and it does not expire.

**Neither one records who has paid you.** The receipt book does that, and it is where a cash plan starts.

## Read the month twice

The first reading is revenue minus costs. It tells you whether the work is worth doing at all, and it is the reading a lender or a parent will ask for.

The second reading is every amount with a date beside it. It tells you which week is tight, and it decides whether you can take a second batch or a bigger room.

A month that passes the first reading and fails the second is still a month you have to get through.
