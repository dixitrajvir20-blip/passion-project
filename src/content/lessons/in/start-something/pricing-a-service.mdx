## What the price has to pay for

Each site needs its own ₹2,600 of domain, hosting and images: a <Term id="variable-cost">variable cost</Term>. The laptop, internet, software and desk cost ₹18,600 a month whatever you sell: <Term id="fixed-cost">fixed costs</Term>.

What a fee keeps after those costs is its <Term id="margin">margin</Term>. The number of sites whose margins cover the fixed costs is <Term id="break-even">break-even</Term>.

So build the price from cost, not a classmate's quote. The job's ₹2,600, plus 40 hours at an example ₹300 to pay you and a share of the month, makes ₹14,600. Quote ₹15,000 as a <Term id="flat-fee">flat fee</Term>, with a written <Term id="scope-of-work">scope of work</Term>.

## Tax the client may take first

Past a set amount, some clients must deduct income tax, <Term id="tds">TDS</Term>, before paying you. Say the clinic deducts ₹1,500 against your <Term id="pan">PAN</Term>: ₹13,500 arrives.

Your fee is still the ₹15,000 on the <Term id="invoice">invoice</Term>. The ₹1,500 is tax paid in your name, listed in your <Term id="annual-information-statement">Annual Information Statement</Term>.
