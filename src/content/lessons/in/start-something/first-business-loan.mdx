## What the certificate is for

<Term id="udyam-registration">Udyam registration</Term> records the coaching centre as an enterprise. It is online and free: nobody is owed a fee for it. An Aadhaar number and a <Term id="pan">PAN</Term> are enough.

The certificate shows the centre is a <Term id="micro-enterprise">micro enterprise</Term>, the smallest size. That is why the statement asks for no <Term id="collateral">collateral</Term>: banks are told to take none on loans up to ₹10 lakh to micro and small enterprises. The lender still decides the loan.

## Three numbers on every loan

Every loan comes down to the <Term id="emi">EMI</Term>, the months and the total. The EMI times the months is the total. The total minus what you borrowed is the price.

For a loan like this, a lender the Reserve Bank regulates must give you a <Term id="key-facts-statement">Key Facts Statement</Term> before you sign. Its <Term id="apr">APR</Term> puts the <Term id="interest">interest</Term> and every charge into one yearly rate.
