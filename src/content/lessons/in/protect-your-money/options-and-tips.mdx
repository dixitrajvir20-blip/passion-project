## Two things only SEBI can allow

<Term id="sebi">SEBI</Term>, India's markets regulator, controls two things. Giving calls or advice on what to trade needs its registration. Claiming results, such as a monthly figure for members, needs its permission. This channel sells calls on <Term id="index-option">index options</Term> and prints a monthly figure: both.

A <Term id="registered-adviser">registered adviser</Term> is on one of SEBI's two public registers, searched by name or number. A number typed into a chat is not a check.

## What one morning hides

In the year to March 2026, 89% of individual traders under 30 in options and futures ended it with a loss. That is the <Term id="base-rate">base rate</Term>: what happened to the whole group.

<Term id="expected-value">Expected value</Term> weighs each result by how often it comes. One big morning is one line in a year. Costs come off every trade, and the fee every month.
