## What the lines mean

<Term id="ctc">CTC</Term> is what the job costs the company for a year. Divided by 12, this offer is ₹35,000 a month. The <Term id="payslip">payslip</Term>'s own total, <Term id="gross-pay">gross salary</Term>, is lower at ₹32,900, because the employer's provident fund share is counted in the CTC and paid straight into your fund.

Two lines then come off. Your <Term id="epf">EPF</Term> share is 12% of <Term id="basic-pay">basic pay</Term>, held in a fund in your name. The employer adds 12% too, and part of that goes to the <Term id="eps">pension scheme</Term>. <Term id="professional-tax">Professional tax</Term> is set by your state. <Term id="tds">TDS</Term> is income tax taken monthly; nil at this pay.

What is left is your <Term id="in-hand-pay">in-hand pay</Term>, ₹30,600. Plan the month on that figure: it is the only one that arrives.
