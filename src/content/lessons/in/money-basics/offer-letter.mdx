## CTC is what you cost

<Term id="ctc">CTC</Term> means cost to company: everything the company expects to spend on you in a year. Some of it is paid to you. Some is paid for you, into a fund, and never passes through your account.

What lands in your account each month is your <Term id="in-hand-pay">in-hand pay</Term>. **CTC ÷ 12 is what you cost each month, not what you get.**

Since the labour codes took effect on 21 November 2025, every worker is due a written appointment letter. Ask for the salary breakup with it. That table lists each part of the CTC, and it is where the gap is explained.

## Two EPF shares, both on basic pay

On a first salary, the biggest cut is often the <Term id="epf">EPF</Term>, the Employees' Provident Fund. You put in 12% of your <Term id="basic-pay">basic pay</Term> each month, and your employer puts in 12% too.

**Both shares are worked out on basic pay, not on the CTC.** In this offer basic is ₹15,000, half the monthly CTC, so each share is ₹1,800.

The employer's share sits inside your CTC but goes straight to the fund. Yours is cut from your salary. Neither is lost: both are kept for you, for later.

If your basic pay is at or under a limit the government sets, EPF is compulsory. On 16 September 2026 the Cabinet approved raising that limit from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month.

## Professional tax and TDS

<Term id="professional-tax">Professional tax</Term> is a tax on jobs set by each state. Your employer takes it from your salary, and the amount depends on your state and your pay. Karnataka's schedule, for example, charges nothing on a monthly salary under ₹25,000. This offer uses ₹200 as an example.

<Term id="tds">TDS</Term>, tax deducted at source, is income tax your employer takes month by month, based on what you are expected to owe for the year. **If no income tax is due for the year, no TDS is taken.** At this pay none is due. The level where tax starts can change each year, so check it on incometax.gov.in.

If tax was taken, your employer gives you Form 16 after the year ends.

## From ₹30,000 to ₹26,200

## Lines that do not arrive monthly

Many letters add lines that make the CTC bigger without adding to any month.

<Term id="variable-pay">Variable pay</Term> depends on targets, and often comes once a year. A joining bonus comes once, and may have to be repaid if you leave early. Health insurance is cover, paid to an insurer, not cash to you.

<Term id="gratuity">Gratuity</Term> is paid when you leave. In a permanent job you need five years of service first. On a fixed-term contract, the labour codes cut that to one year.

**Plan your month on fixed in-hand pay.** Your <Term id="budget">budget</Term> starts there. Anything else is a possible extra, and it gets its own plan when it lands.
