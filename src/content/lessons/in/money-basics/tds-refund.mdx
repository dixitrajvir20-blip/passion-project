## Withheld is not owed

<Term id="tds">TDS</Term>, tax deducted at source, comes off a payment before it reaches you. The payer deposits it against your <Term id="pan">PAN</Term>, as tax paid in your name in advance.

The real bill is worked out once, on the year's total. Under the <Term id="new-tax-regime">new tax regime</Term>, the <Term id="tax-slab">tax slabs</Term> charge nothing on the first ₹4,00,000 and 5% from there to ₹8,00,000. A <Term id="tax-rebate">tax rebate</Term> then clears the tax on taxable income up to ₹12,00,000. Up to that income, everything withheld comes back.

## Where it shows, and how it returns

<Term id="form-26as">Form 26AS</Term> and the <Term id="annual-information-statement">Annual Information Statement</Term> list each deduction against your PAN. Only credits on record are refunded, and only the payer can add a missing one.

A <Term id="tax-return">tax return</Term> for that year claims the refund. It is processed once you verify it online, and paid into a bank account the portal has validated.
