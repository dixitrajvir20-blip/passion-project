## Withheld is not the same as owed

<Term id="tds">TDS</Term> means tax deducted at source. The payer takes tax off before paying you and deposits it with the government against your <Term id="pan">PAN</Term>.

It is an advance, worked out payment by payment. The payer does not know what your income for the whole year will come to.

**The real bill is worked out once, on the year, by your <Term id="tax-return">tax return</Term>.** Where the advance came to more than the bill, the difference comes back. Where it came to less, the rest is paid then.

Tax deducted on income for the 2025-26 financial year shows in the statement for the <Term id="assessment-year">assessment year</Term> that follows, and is claimed in that year's return.

## Why the tax due is nil

Tax is worked out on the year's total income, not on any one payment.

Under the new regime for the 2025-26 financial year, the department's table starts tax above ₹4,00,000. A <Term id="tax-rebate">tax rebate</Term> of up to ₹60,000 then applies where taxable income does not exceed ₹12,00,000.

**At ₹1,50,000 of income for the year, the rebate leaves nothing to pay.** The amount already deposited has no bill to be set against.

The rules change from year to year, and other income counts towards the same total. Check the current table on incometax.gov.in before working out your own figure.

## Where to see what was withheld

Two statements show tax withheld in your name, and both sit inside your account on the tax portal.

<Term id="form-26as">Form 26AS</Term> is the tax credit statement. The <Term id="annual-information-statement">Annual Information Statement</Term> shows the same deductions alongside other transactions reported about you, and it takes your feedback where an entry looks wrong.

**Check both against your own record of every payment before filing.** A missing quarter usually means the payer's own filing is incomplete, and only the payer can correct that.

## What a return does, and when

A return is the step that turns a credit on record into money in your account.

You report the year's income, set the tax already deducted against the bill, and ask for the difference. **A refund is worked out only after the return has been verified, so the last step counts as much as the first.**

The account it is paid into has to be pre-validated on the portal and match your PAN. Departmental guidance puts a refund at roughly four to five weeks after processing.

For assessment year 2026-27 the due date was 31 July 2026. A belated return for that year may still be filed on or before 31 December 2026, and a delayed-filing fee can apply.

## When nothing is claimed

Money withheld and never claimed stays with the government. Nothing arrives to prompt you, because a credit on record is not a request for a refund.

**The habit that prevents it is small: keep every invoice and payment note for the year in one folder, and check the two statements once the year closes.**

A first year of paid work often has one payer and one credit to check. The same ten minutes covers four payers later, which is when forgetting one starts to cost more.
