## Why a month of your own costs

A <Term id="emergency-fund">buffer</Term> is one month of your own costs, kept where you can reach it this week. On the statement that is ₹5,500, passed on your third payment of ₹2,000.

Money in an account can earn <Term id="interest">interest</Term> on what you put in, the <Term id="principal">principal</Term>. Leave the interest in, and the next round is worked out on a larger balance: <Term id="compound-growth">compound growth</Term>.

## The same maths on what you owe

Borrow the ₹3,600 at an example 36% a year, charged as 3% a month, and repay nothing. A year later you owe about ₹5,130. One flat 36% charge would make it ₹4,896. The gap is each month's 3% charged on earlier interest too.

Before a personal loan, a lender the Reserve Bank regulates must give you a <Term id="key-facts-statement">Key Facts Statement</Term> with the <Term id="apr">APR</Term> and every charge.
