## Where each month goes

Your <Term id="epf">EPF</Term> shares are worked out on <Term id="basic-pay">basic pay</Term>. You put in 12%, and so does your employer. The employer's 12% is split: 8.33% of basic pay goes to the <Term id="employees-pension-scheme">Employees' Pension Scheme</Term>. The other 3.67% joins your 12% in the balance, under a <Term id="uan">UAN</Term> that follows you to every job.

## Transfer or take it out

A <Term id="transfer-claim">transfer claim</Term> moves the balance into the new account. It stays out of your income, and the old months join your <Term id="continuous-service">continuous service</Term>.

Taking it all out is only for time out of work, not a job move. Before five years it usually counts as income, and at ₹50,000 or more, 10% comes off first as <Term id="tds">TDS</Term>.
