Our mission

Most financial and business education assumes you already have something: a family business, an adviser, a network, or spare money to experiment with. Millions of young people have none of those things — and the advice that reaches them is usually selling something.

LaunchPad exists to close that gap. Everything here is free, written in plain language, and designed to make sense whether you live in Mumbai, Lagos, São Paulo or Ohio. We don't sell products, we don't take commissions, and we don't pretend that getting ahead is simple.
