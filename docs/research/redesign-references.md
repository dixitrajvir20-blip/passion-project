# Redesign references: what the tabs showed (22 September 2026)

Rajvir, looking at the live site in Chrome with his Mac in dark mode, saw every page painted
dark blue and asked for the opposite: the whole page white, the header the only blue block, blue
for accents, "as professional as possible and simple to follow", and no more of the
Academy-style lesson cards. These are the notes from opening the reference sites in a browser
tab and looking, not reading about them.

## Zerodha Varsity (zerodha.com/varsity) — the closest fit for India and for the tone
- White page, near-black text, one blue for links and the single button. No second colour.
- Header: a small logo and six plain text links. No pills, no boxes.
- Home: a large plain headline, one paragraph, one small blue button, then "Explore Varsity".
- Chapter page: a module label with a thin blue underline, the chapter title, "Chapters · Next"
  on the right, then a narrow reading column with numbered H2s ("1.1 – Why should I invest?").
- Tables: a dark header row, thin rules, right-aligned figures. Hand-drawn illustrations are the
  only decoration and sit inside the column.

## Khan Academy (financial literacy course)
- A dark navy header bar over a white page; white text and a green logo in the bar.
- Course page: a left sidebar listing units ("Unit 1 · Welcome to Financial Literacy"), a grid
  of exercise boxes per unit, one card "Course challenge" with a thin border.
- Everything is boxes with 1px borders and small labels; no colour blocks in the content area.

## CFPB consumer tools (consumerfinance.gov)
- White page, a small green logo, a text nav with dropdowns, a thin green rule under the nav.
- A pale hero with a heading, one paragraph and an illustration; then plain lists of links
  in three columns, H2s in a serif-free grotesque, generous line spacing.
- The whole page is type and rules; the only colour is the link blue and the logo green.

## GOV.UK (student finance guide)
- One blue bar with the crown logo and Menu/Search; a pale "Part of" band; a black H1 at
  large size; a "Contents" list with dashes; body text at 19px in a 2/3 column.
- No cards, no icons, no decoration. The most "simple to follow" page of the set.

## Investopedia (a term page)
- White page, black display headline, a byline block with "Reviewed by / Fact checked by",
  then the article. Heavy advertising around it, which is the thing not to copy.

## Chase Money Skills (budget page)
- A blue full-width hero band with the page title in white, then a tab row (Overview,
  Budgeting, Credit…), then white sections of two columns with a heading and a paragraph;
  a second blue band introduces "Understand your spending". Blue is used as bands, not tints.
- Photographs of people and worksheet thumbnails; not for us (no stock photos).

## What the set has in common (the direction)
- White pages; ink text; one blue, used for the header bar, links and the primary button.
- Plain text navigation, no pill groups; a thin rule to separate the header from the page.
- Reading pages are one narrow column with clear numbered headings and a contents list.
- Course pages are a list or a sidebar of units, each a line of text with a number; no cards
  with stickers or minute pills.
- Tables and figures carry the data; decoration is absent or hand-drawn and inside the column.
- No gold panels, no rounded 32px blocks, no hand-lettered notes, no blobs, no dark theme.
