# Regional teaching research: Brazil, Nigeria, Kenya, Gulf/MENA, SE Asia (17 Sep 2026)

**Method note.** Country/youth figures are pulled from the World Bank Global Findex 2025 database (source 28, data year 2024): https://databank.worldbank.org/source/global-findex-database. "Youth" = ages 15–24. Items marked **[unverified]** could not be fetched from a primary page.

## 0. Cross-cutting evidence: what works
- Kaiser, Lusardi, Menkhoff & Urban (2022, JFE): 76 RCTs, >160,000 people; positive effects on knowledge and behaviour, cost-effective. https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v145y2022i2p255-272.html
- Kaiser & Menkhoff (2020): 37 studies, 115,000 students; +0.33 SD knowledge, +0.07 SD behaviour. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0272775718306940
- Fernandes, Lynch & Netemeyer (2014): interventions explain 0.1% of behaviour variance; recommend "just-in-time" education. https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ormnsc/v60y2014i8p1861-1883.html
- Brazil high-school RCT (Bruhn et al. 2016): 892 schools, ~25,000 students; +0.25 SD proficiency, better saving/budgeting, but more expensive-credit use short-term. https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2Fapp.20150149. **Nine-year follow-up** (BCB WP 563, 2022): effects persisted; treated students less likely to borrow from expensive sources, +10% formal microenterprise (MEI) ownership. https://www.bcb.gov.br/pec/wps/ingl/wps563.pdf
- Rules of thumb beat accounting for micro-entrepreneurs (Drexler, Fischer & Schoar 2014). https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/app.6.2.1
- Edutainment works (Berg & Zia 2017): a South African soap-opera storyline nearly doubled formal borrowing, cut gambling. https://ideas.repec.org/p/wbk/wbrwps/6407.html
- Design implications: short, decision-adjacent modules; heuristics; narrative characters; simulations; explicit credit-cost lessons; warm hand-offs to a real first action.

## 1. Brazil
**Inclusion/mobile (Findex 2025):** adults 86.4%; youth 84.4%; youth digital payment 81.3%; smartphone 88%; 23.9% of youth received a scam call/SMS; top worry = monthly bills (44.6%).
**Strategy/curriculum:** Nova ENEF (Decreto 10.393, 2020) https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2019-2022/2020/decreto/D10393.htm. BCB's *Aprender Valor* integrates financial education into BNCC subjects https://www.bcb.gov.br/conteudo/home-ptbr/TextosApresentacoes/MM_Aprender_Valor.pdf; 25,000 schools, 5M+ students (Aug 2025); expands to ensino médio in 2026 https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2025/cvm-anbima-e-sebrae-firmam-parceria-com-o-banco-central-para-levar-educacao-financeira-a-docentes-e-estudantes-do-ensino-medio
**Rails/informal:** Pix >175M users (secondary: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pix_(payment_system)). Consórcio 13.41M participants Jul 2026 https://abac.org.br/
**Macro:** credit-card *rotativo* 428.3% a.a. (Mar 2026); household indebtedness 49.9% (Feb 2026) https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/economia/money/macroeconomia/juros-do-rotativo-do-cartao-cai-a-4283-em-marco-uso-da-modalidade-sobe/
**Formats:** rotativo simulator; MEI path explainer (brigadeiro/açaí seller → formalisation); Pix-scam "spot the fake comprovante" quiz. Examples: brigadeiro seller, açaí cart, motoboy, pet-sitting via Pix. Portuguese, LTR.

## 2. Nigeria

**Rails:** PalmPay 35M users; Moniepoint. Informal: ajo/esusu/adashe.
**Macro:** inflation 15.39% Aug 2026 (vs 23.14% Aug 2025) https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/15/nigerias-headline-inflation-eases-to-15-39-in-august/
**Language:** English official; Hausa ~63M, Yoruba ~46M, Igbo ~33M, Nigerian Pidgin 60–80M.
**Formats:** Pidgin-first, text-light pages ≤50 KB with audio toggle and printables; "Ajo ledger" worked example + POS agent economics; inflation "price of a plate of rice" mini-sim. Examples: suya stand, POS agent kiosk, okada/keke, phone-charging/data-reselling, tailoring.

## Key caveats
UAE absent from Findex 2025; Brazil/Saudi "mobile money" reflects Findex 2025's broadened definition; Egypt CBE vs Findex use different definitions; several official scam pages (BCB, CBK, CBUAE, SAMA, OJK) unreachable; Wikipedia used for payment-app and informal-group figures where regulator pages were JS-only.
