# Region-specific teaching research: India, US, EU, UK (as of 17 Sep 2026)

Method: web research only; primary/official sources preferred; every claim has a URL. Items not verified are flagged at the end.

## 1. INDIA

**Frameworks**
- NSFE 2020–25 (NCFE, with RBI/SEBI/IRDAI/PFRDA) is being succeeded by **NSFE 3.0 (2026–31)**, "currently in development" — https://ncfe.org.in/nsfe3-suggestions/ ; NSFE 2020–25: https://ncfe.org.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/NSFE_20-25_ENG.pdf ; mid-term evaluation 2025: https://ncfe.org.in/mte-nsfe-2025/
- **Money Smart School Programme**: Classes VI–X, five NCFE–CBSE workbooks, teacher training — https://ncfe.org.in/mssp/
- **CBSE + NPCI Class VI financial-literacy elective** (banking, savings, UPI/IMPS/QR, security) — https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Curriculum22/publication/middle/financial_Literacy_classVI.pdf
- **RBI Financial Literacy Week**: 2026 "KYC – Your First Step to Safe Banking" (9–13 Feb 2026; warns on fake-KYC links and money mules) — https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2225103&reg=48&lang=2
- **SEBI investor education**: https://investor.sebi.gov.in/ ; Saa₹thi 2.0 app.

**Money situations that hit 15–21s**
- Derivatives gambling: under-30s were **43% of individual F&O traders in FY26** and **89% of them lost money** (SEBI study, 23 Aug 2026) — https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/derivatives-market-gets-gen-z-twist-but-losses-cast-a-long-shadow-sebi-126082300682_1.html
- Only 9.5% of households invest in securities; 79% of Gen-Z households risk-averse; non-investors want "relatable role models" (SEBI Investor Survey, 30 Sep 2025) — https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/indian-households-including-gen-z-remain-risk-averse-sebi-survey-125093001479_1.html
- First job: CTC vs in-hand (EPF, professional tax, TDS) is a standard confusion.

**Payment rails/products**
- **UPI**: record 24.51 bn transactions, ₹29.82 trn in Aug 2026 (791 mn/day) — https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/upi-transactions-august-2026-record-volume-npci-126090100506_1.html
- Small-savings rates Jul–Sep 2026: **PPF 7.1%**, 5-yr Post Office RD 6.7%, NSC 7.7%, SSY 8.2% — https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/investment/story/govt-keeps-small-savings-interest-rates-unchanged-for-july-september-quarter-check-latest-ppf-scss-ssy-rates-540090-2026-06-30

**Scams (official numbers)**
- NCRP complaints 22.68 lakh in 2024; **losses ₹22,845.73 crore in 2024** (vs ₹7,465 cr 2023); ₹7,130 cr saved via 1930/CFCFRMS; 24.67 lakh mule accounts flagged (Lok Sabha, 2 Dec 2025) — https://www.mha.gov.in/MHA1/Par2017/pdfs/par2025-pdfs/LS02122025/432.pdf
- PIB backgrounder on fraud types (spoofing, deepfakes, UPI fraud, betting apps) — https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/oct/doc2025108660701.pdf
- RBI "BE(A)WARE" booklet — https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/PressRelease/PDFs/PR1817F4A7B3662BEC4AD7AC6A2D85EAF937ED.PDF

**Learning habits/constraints**
- IAMAI–Kantar Internet in India 2025 (30 Jan 2026): **958 mn** active users, **57% rural**, 61% watch short video, **18% go online via someone else's phone** — https://bestmediainfo.com/insights/indias-internet-users-near-one-billion-in-2025-rural-india-leads-growth-iamai-11056899
- 2024 edition: **98% accessed content in Indic languages; 57% of urban users prefer Indic content**; only **24 mn online learning** — https://www.iamai.in/sites/default/files/research/Kantar_%20IAMAI%20report_2024_.pdf

**Help links**: https://cybercrime.gov.in/ (1930) ; https://sachet.rbi.org.in/ ; https://cms.rbi.org.in/ ; https://scores.sebi.gov.in/ ; https://ncfe.org.in/

**Regulatory limits (verified wording)**
- SEBI circular 29 Jan 2025: regulated persons must ensure anyone associated does not (i) give advice on securities without registration or (ii) claim returns. "A person engaged solely in education shall mean that such person is not engaged in any of the two prohibited activities." — https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jan-2025/details-clarifications-on-provisions-related-to-association-of-persons-regulated-by-the-board-miis-and-their-agents-with-persons-engaged-in-prohibited-activities_91356.html (parent circular 22 Oct 2024)
- **Updated for the lag period**: SEBI circular of **8 May 2026** ("Norms for sharing and usage of price data for educational purposes"), effective **1 Jul 2026**, sets a uniform **30-day lag** for education-only entities — https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/may-2026/norms-for-sharing-and-usage-of-price-data-for-educational-purposes_101293.html
- Practical rule: never name a security with a price/target; use ≥30-day-old or fictional data; no "returns" claims; no affiliate ties to unregistered tipsters.

**Interactive formats (India)**: (1) UPI "Spot the Fake" simulator; (2) offer-letter decoder (CTC → in-hand); (3) chai/tiffin-stall P&L game; (4) "tips group" trap; (5) PPF vs RD vs FD vs SIP goal ladder. Analogies: kirana udhaar, chit funds, gold as family savings, Diwali bonus.

## 2. UNITED STATES

**Frameworks**
- **National Standards for Personal Financial Education (2021, Jump$tart + CEE)**: six areas — https://www.councilforeconed.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/2021-National-Standards-for-Personal-Financial-Education.pdf
- **NGPF tracker: 30 "guarantee" states** require a standalone personal-finance course (dashboard 16 Sep 2026) — https://www.ngpf.org/live-us-dashboard/ ; 76% of public HS students (Class of 2031) covered once rolled out — https://www.ngpf.org/blog/advocacy/how-many-states-require-students-to-take-a-personal-finance-course-for-high-school-graduation/
- **CFPB Building Blocks**: ages 13–21 = solidifying knowledge/skills — https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/youth-financial-education/learn/

**Money situations**
- **Student loans from 1 Jul 2026** (OBBBA): Grad PLUS closed to new borrowers; caps; new Repayment Assistance Plan — https://www.cbsnews.com/news/student-loan-changes-july-1-2026/
- **BNPL**: 37% of 18–24s used BNPL; 63% of borrowers had multiple simultaneous loans (CFPB, Jan 2025) — https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_BNPL_Report_2025_01.pdf ; **FICO Score 10 BNPL** (Jun 2025) — https://investors.fico.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fico-unveils-groundbreaking-credit-scores-incorporate-buy-now
- **Gig/side-hustle tax**: IRS gig economy center — https://www.irs.gov/businesses/gig-economy-tax-center
- **Finfluencers**: FINRA Foundation (2 Apr 2026): followers skew young/male, lower objective knowledge but higher self-rated knowledge.

**Payment rails**: FTC (Jun 2024) — payment-app scam median loss $380 vs $136 cards; PayPal 28%, Cash App 24%, Zelle 20%, Venmo 9% — https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/fraud-scam-ftc-federal-trade-commission-payment-app-paypal-cash-zelle-venmo-apple-pay/717923/

**Scams**
- 2025: **3 mn fraud reports, $15.9 bn lost**; investment scams $7.9 bn (FTC, 25 Mar 2026) — https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/ftc-testifies-joint-economic-committee-agencys-efforts-combat-fraud
- Social-media-originated losses **$2.1 bn in 2025** (27 Apr 2026) — https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/04/new-ftc-data-show-people-have-lost-billions-social-media-scams
- Young adults lose to job scams at 5× and investment scams at 4× the older rate (FTC spotlight, Dec 2022) — https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2022/12/who-experiences-scams-story-all-ages

**Help links**: https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/ ; https://www.identitytheft.gov/ ; https://www.consumerfinance.gov/complaint/ ; https://www.investor.gov/ ; https://brokercheck.finra.org/

**Regulatory limits**: FINRA influencer sweep → 8 enforcement actions (https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/targeted-examination-letters/social-media-influencers-customer-acquisition-related-information-protection); SEC IAC finfluencer recommendation (22 Nov 2024) — https://www.sec.gov/files/sec-iac-finfluencer-recommendation-11222024.pdf

**Interactive formats (US)**: first-paycheck decoder; BNPL basket simulator; student-loan translator; credit-score sandbox; side-hustle tax set-aside. Analogies: lemonade stand → food truck, sneaker resale, lawn-care LLC, Roth IRA "first-job superpower".

## 3. EUROPEAN UNION

**Frameworks**

- **EU Financial Literacy Strategy, COM(2025) 681, 30 Sep 2025**: 18% high financial literacy; 49% of 18–65s lack 3 months' savings; ambassadors from Q1 2026; EU-wide campaign incl. scam prevention — https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:52025DC0681
- **PISA 2022 financial literacy** (Jun 2024): Flemish Belgium 527, Denmark 521, Netherlands 517, Czechia 507, Austria 506, Poland 506, US 505, Portugal 494, Hungary 492, Spain 486, Italy 484; OECD avg 498 — https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/06/pisa-2022-results-volume-iv_125a58b3/5a849c2a-en.pdf

**Payment rails**: **Instant Payments Regulation** – receive from 9 Jan 2025, send + verification of payee from 9 Oct 2025 — https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/retail/instant_payments/html/instant_payments_regulation.en.html ; **Wero** (EPI) live in BE/FR/DE — https://wero-wallet.eu/

**Scams**: Europol — ">90% of money mule transactions ... are linked to cybercrime" — https://www.europol.europa.eu/operations-services-and-innovation/public-awareness-and-prevention-guides/money-muling

**Learning constraints**: 24 official languages; 49.4% of upper-secondary students in vocational tracks (2024) — https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Vocational_education_statistics

**Help links**: IT https://www.quellocheconta.gov.it/ ; ES https://www.finanzasparatodos.es/ ; FR https://www.mesquestionsdargent.fr/ ; NL https://www.wijzeringeldzaken.nl/ ; https://www.eccnet.eu/ ; https://www.esma.europa.eu/investor-corner/frauds-and-scams

**Regulatory limits**: ESMA statement on social-media investment recommendations under MAR (ESMA70-154-2780, 28 Oct 2021; text not re-verified). Retail Investment Strategy finfluencer rules: status unverified.

**Interactive formats (EU)**: apprentice payslip decoder with country presets; SEPA Instant/Wero name-check drill; "don't be a mule" job-ad classifier; crypto/gamified-app risk meter; market-stall or Erasmus-budget simulator.
